3. 10+ Tailwind CSS UI Components Breakdown (Per Page)

1. Home (`index.html`) 10 Sticky Navbar, Hero Container, Primary Button, Outline Button, Alert Info Banner, 4-Card Stats Grid, 3-Card Focus Area Grid, Quote Box, FAQ Accordion (`<details>`), 3-Column Footer.
