Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join AXON at UET Lahore? ▼

Any undergraduate student enrolled in IMAC, Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related departments at UET Lahore is welcome to join.

Are workshops and bootcamps free? ▼

Yes, all hands-on workshops, bootcamps, and coding seminars hosted by AXON are completely free of cost for UET students.

Can beginners with zero coding experience join? ▼

Yes! We have beginner-friendly roadmaps for freshers starting with basic HTML/CSS and introductory Python.
